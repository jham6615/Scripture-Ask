Bible for Seekers Support

Read the Bible in dozens of translations and ask anything about any passage. We're glad you're here — here's how to get help.

Contact us

Email shuttlementoradm@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. Please include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

Common questions

  • How do I change translations? Tap the version label under the book name at the top, then pick a language and translation.
  • How does the free plan work? Reading the Bible is always free. You can ask the AI one question for free; after that, Bible for Seekers Premium (Yearly or Monthly) unlocks unlimited questions.
  • How do I manage or cancel my subscription? On your iPhone, open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Bible for Seekers.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Sign in to your Bible for Seekers account, then open the Premium screen and tap "Restore purchases."
  • How do I delete my account? Email us and we'll remove your account and data.
